⚠ Important safety information — read before use

This tool does not diagnose Lassa fever. It provides probability estimates to support clinical decision-making. All results require laboratory RT-PCR confirmation. Probability estimates are based on published clinical literature and must not replace clinical judgment.

Report any suspected case to NCDC immediately — do not wait for laboratory results before notifying.
